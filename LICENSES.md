## Music
- `audio/summertime.mp3`: "Summertime" by My Chemical Romance, from *Danger Days: The True Lives of the Fabulous Killjoys* (Reprise Records, 2010). Copyright the band and label. Sean supplied it, and it is used here only as "our song" in a private gift. It was leveled to about −16 LUFS with ffmpeg `loudnorm`, and its cover art was removed.
- `audio/summertime-piano.mp3`: an easy-piano cover of "Summertime" that Sean supplied. The composition is copyright My Chemical Romance, and the performance belongs to its arranger. The 8.45 s of leading silence was trimmed and the track was leveled to about −16 LUFS.
- Lyrics shown in the ending (up to the end of the second chorus) are copyright My Chemical Romance. The line timings come from Sean's timing file and are embedded in `Forest_Preview.html`.
- Publishing these files on a public site (for example GitHub Pages) can draw a takedown request. Sean chose to accept that risk.
